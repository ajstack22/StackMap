Privacy Policy

StackMap Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2025

Your privacy is important to us. StackMap is designed with privacy at its core. We believe your data belongs to you and should stay on your device.

🔒 Our Privacy Promise

StackMap operates with a simple principle: All your data stays on your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.

📱 Data Collection

We don't collect any data. Specifically:

  • No personal information
  • No usage analytics
  • No behavior tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No crash reports sent to us

💾 Data Storage

All StackMap data is stored locally on your device using secure storage methods:

  • Activity data is saved in your device's secure storage
  • Settings and preferences remain on your device
  • Images and icons are stored locally
  • No cloud synchronization or external backups

👶 Children's Privacy

StackMap is designed to be safe for users of all ages. Since we don't collect any data, children's privacy is inherently protected. Parents and guardians can feel confident that their children's information remains private.

🌐 Third-Party Services

StackMap does not integrate with any third-party services that could collect data. There are:

  • No analytics services
  • No advertising networks
  • No social media integrations
  • No external APIs

✅ Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

  • Delete any data at any time through the app
  • Export your data if needed
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data
  • No account or registration required

📖 Open Source

StackMap is committed to transparency. Our code is open source, allowing anyone to verify our privacy practices and contribute to making the app better for everyone.

Questions about our privacy policy? Contact us at privacy@stackmap.app
