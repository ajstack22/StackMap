## Store Listing Requirements

### 1. App Information

**App Name**: StackMap

**Short Description** (80 chars max):
"Organize your thoughts and knowledge with visual mind mapping"

**Full Description** (4000 chars max):
```
StackMap is your personal knowledge management companion that helps you capture, organize, and connect your ideas visually. Whether you're a student, researcher, or knowledge worker, StackMap transforms how you think and learn.

KEY FEATURES:
• Visual Mind Mapping - Create beautiful, interconnected knowledge maps
• Quick Capture - Save ideas, notes, and insights instantly
• Smart Organization - Automatically categorize and tag your content
• Powerful Search - Find any piece of information in seconds
• Offline Access - Work anywhere, anytime, even without internet
• Cross-Device Sync - Access your knowledge base from any device
• Privacy First - Your data stays secure and private

PERFECT FOR:
• Students organizing study materials
• Researchers managing sources and citations
• Writers developing ideas and outlines
• Professionals tracking projects and tasks
• Anyone building a personal knowledge base

WHY STACKMAP:
Unlike traditional note-taking apps, StackMap uses visual connections to help you see relationships between ideas. Build your second brain and never lose a thought again.

Start free and upgrade when you need advanced features like unlimited storage, team collaboration, and priority support.

Join thousands of users who've transformed their thinking with StackMap. Download now and start building your knowledge empire!
```
