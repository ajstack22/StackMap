StackMap: Visual Routines Made Simple

Transform daily chaos into calm with StackMap - the visual routine builder designed for families with special needs children, but loved by all families seeking structure and independence.

🌟 WHY FAMILIES LOVE STACKMAP

Built with input from families of children with autism, ADHD, and other developmental differences, StackMap turns abstract schedules into concrete, visual steps that kids can understand and follow independently.

📱 KEY FEATURES

✓ VISUAL ACTIVITY CARDS
Create colorful cards with emojis and simple descriptions. No reading required - perfect for non-readers and visual learners.

✓ PERSONALIZED ROUTINES
Each family member gets their own customized schedule. Different activities for different days - because Tuesday isn't Thursday!

✓ ONE-TAP COMPLETION
Kids tap cards to mark activities complete. Simple, satisfying, and builds independence with every tap.

✓ GROWN-UP MODE
Protected editing keeps routines safe from accidental changes. Only grown-ups can modify schedules.

✓ CARD LIBRARY
Choose from pre-made activity cards or create your own. Morning routines, bedtime rituals, therapy schedules - we've got you covered.

✓ WORKS OFFLINE
No internet? No problem. StackMap works anywhere, anytime. Optional Google Drive sync keeps data safe across devices.

✓ MULTIPLE THEMES
Choose colors that work for your family. High contrast options for better visibility.

• Bedtime rituals that stick
