# StackMap Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: June 20, 2025*  
*Effective Date: June 20, 2025*

## Our Privacy Commitment

StackMap is designed with families in mind, especially those with special needs children. We believe your privacy is sacred, and we've built our app to reflect that belief.

**THE SIMPLE VERSION: We don't collect, store, or see your data. Period.**

## What Information We Do NOT Collect

StackMap is unique because of what we DON'T do:

- ❌ NO personal information collection
- ❌ NO email addresses or names
- ❌ NO usage analytics or tracking
- ❌ NO location data
- ❌ NO device identifiers
- ❌ NO behavioral tracking
- ❌ NO third-party analytics
- ❌ NO advertising networks
- ❌ NO data sales to anyone, ever

## How StackMap Works

### Local Storage Only
All your data (routines, activities, user profiles) is stored locally on your device using:
- **iOS**: Core Data / Local Storage
- **Android**: Local SQLite Database
- **Web**: Browser's IndexedDB

This means:
- Your data never leaves your device unless YOU choose to sync it
- Deleting the app removes all data
- We have no servers storing your information
- We cannot recover lost data (please use backups!)

### Optional Google Drive Sync
If you choose to enable Google Drive synchronization:
- You sign in with YOUR Google account
- Data is stored in YOUR Google Drive
- We never see or access your Google credentials
- We never see the data you sync
- You can disable sync anytime
- This is completely optional

## Information You Control

### What's Stored Locally:
- User profiles (names and avatar colors only)
- Activity cards (emoji and text descriptions)
- Daily routines and schedules
- Completion status
- App preferences (theme, settings)
- Custom activity library

### Data Sharing:
- **Export**: Creates a local file you can share manually
- **Import**: Accepts routine files you provide
- **No automatic sharing**: All sharing requires your explicit action

## Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

StackMap takes children's privacy seriously:

### We Never:
- Collect information from children
- Request personal details
- Enable social features or chat
- Allow user-generated content sharing
- Display behavioral advertising
- Track children's usage patterns

### Parents Control Everything:
- All settings require "Grown-up Mode"
- No account creation needed
- No age verification required
- Complete parental control over all features

## Data Security

### How We Protect Your Privacy:
- **No data transmission**: Data stays on your device
- **No servers**: We don't operate servers that could be hacked
- **No accounts**: No passwords to be stolen
- **Encrypted sync**: Google Drive uses Google's encryption
- **Local control**: You manage your own backups

### Your Security Responsibilities:
- Keep your device password-protected
- Use device encryption (built into modern iOS/Android)
- Secure your Google account if using sync
- Make regular backups of important data

## Your Privacy Rights

You have absolute control over your data:

### Right to Access
- All your data is directly accessible in the app
- Export feature provides complete data download

### Right to Delete
- Delete specific items within the app
- Uninstall the app to remove all data
- Clear browser data (for web version)

### Right to Portability
- Export your data anytime
- Standard JSON format for easy transfer
- Import into any StackMap installation

### Right to Restrict
- Use StackMap without sync
- Disable any optional features
- Complete offline functionality
