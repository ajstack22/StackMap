# StackMap Privacy Policy

*Last updated: December 2024*

## Our Commitment to Your Privacy

StackMap is designed with families in mind, especially families with special needs children. We understand the importance of privacy and data security. This policy explains how StackMap handles your information with complete transparency.

## The Short Version

- **Your data stays on your device** - We don't collect or store your routines
- **No account required** - Use StackMap without signing up
- **No tracking** - We don't track your usage or behavior
- **No ads** - StackMap is completely ad-free
- **Optional backup only** - Google Drive sync is entirely optional and uses your own account

## Information We Don't Collect

StackMap is a local-first application, which means:

- We don't collect personal information
- We don't store your routines on our servers
- We don't track your location
- We don't use analytics or tracking tools
- We don't require user accounts
- We don't send your data to third parties

## How StackMap Works

### Local Storage
All your data (routines, activities, user profiles) is stored locally on your device using your web browser's local storage. This means:
- Your data never leaves your device unless you choose to sync
- You have complete control over your information
- Clearing your browser data will remove StackMap data

### Optional Google Drive Sync
If you choose to enable Google Drive sync:
- You sign in with your own Google account
- StackMap creates a folder in your Google Drive
- Your routines are backed up to your personal cloud storage
- We never see or access your Google Drive data
- You can disable sync at any time

## Data You Control

### What's Stored Locally:
- User profiles (names and colors)
- Activity cards (emoji, descriptions)
- Daily routines and schedules
- Completion status
- Theme preferences
- Card library selections

### Sharing Data:
- Export feature creates a file you can share manually
- Import feature accepts files you provide
- All sharing is under your direct control

## Children's Privacy

StackMap is designed to be used by children with parental supervision:
- We don't collect information from children
- No social features or communication tools
- No user-generated content sharing
- Parents control all data through Grown-up Mode

## Security Measures

- All data is stored in your browser's secure storage
- Optional Google Drive sync uses Google's security
- No data transmission to StackMap servers
- Export files are created locally on your device

## Your Rights

You always have the right to:
- Use StackMap without providing any personal information
- Delete all data by clearing browser storage
- Export your data at any time
- Choose not to use cloud sync
- Stop using StackMap without any data retention

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date.

## Open Source Transparency

StackMap is open source software. You can review our code to verify our privacy practices at any time.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or StackMap's privacy practices:

- GitHub: [StackMap Repository](https://github.com/ajstack22/StackMap)
- Submit an issue on GitHub for privacy concerns

## Compliance

StackMap is designed to comply with:
- GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
- COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
- CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)

By using a local-first approach, we minimize data collection and maximize user privacy.
