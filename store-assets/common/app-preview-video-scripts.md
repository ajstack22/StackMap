# StackMap App Preview Video Scripts

*Last Updated: June 20, 2025*

## Video Specifications

### iOS App Preview
- **Duration**: 15-30 seconds
- **Resolution**: 1920×886 (iPhone 6.5"), 2732×2048 (iPad 12.9")
- **Format**: .mov, .m4v, or .mp4
- **Frame rate**: 30 fps
- **Audio**: Optional but recommended

### Google Play Promo Video
- **Duration**: 30 seconds - 2 minutes
- **Resolution**: 1920×1080 minimum
- **Format**: .mp4
- **Orientation**: Landscape preferred
- **Audio**: Highly recommended

## Production Guidelines

### Visual Style
- **Colors**: Bright, friendly, accessible
- **Fonts**: Large, clear, easy to read
- **Transitions**: Smooth, not jarring
- **Icons**: Consistent with app design

### Audio Guidelines
- **Music**: Uplifting but calm
- **Voiceover**: Warm, parent-friendly tone
- **Sound effects**: Minimal, pleasant
- **Volume**: Accessible levels

### Accessibility Considerations
- **Captions**: Always included
- **Contrast**: High contrast text
- **Pacing**: Not too fast
- **Visual clarity**: Avoid clutter

### Device Mockups
- **iPhone**: Latest models preferred
- **iPad**: Show for classroom scenes
- **Android**: Include for diversity
- **Real hands**: Show actual interaction
