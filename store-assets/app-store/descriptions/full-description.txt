Transform daily routines into visual success stories with StackMap - the picture schedule app designed for children with autism, ADHD, and other special needs.

VISUAL LEARNING MADE SIMPLE
StackMap turns abstract daily tasks into concrete visual steps. Using emojis and colorful cards, children can see their day at a glance and tap to complete activities independently.

FEATURES THAT MAKE A DIFFERENCE

• Picture-Based Schedules: Create routines using emojis and images - no reading required
• Individual Profiles: Each family member gets personalized routines
• Day-Specific Activities: Different schedules for different days
• One-Tap Completion: Kids mark activities done with a simple tap
• Protected Editing: Grown-up mode prevents accidental changes
• Activity Library: Pre-made cards for common routines
• Offline First: Works without internet connection
• Cloud Backup: Optional Google Drive sync
• Customizable Themes: Choose colors that work for your child

BUILT FOR SPECIAL NEEDS
Every detail is designed with sensory and processing differences in mind:
- Large, clear touch targets
- High contrast visuals
- Predictable interactions
- No overwhelming animations
- Calm, supportive design
