App Icon Specifications

  • iOS App Store: 1024 × 1024 pixels (no transparency, no rounded corners)
  • Google Play: 512 × 512 pixels (32-bit PNG with alpha)
  • Corner radius: Applied automatically by the stores
  • Safe zone: Keep important elements within 80% of icon area
  • Colors: Use high contrast, avoid pure black or white backgrounds
  • Testing: Preview at small sizes (20px) to ensure clarity

📱 Exporting App Icons

To create production-ready app icons:

  1. Choose your preferred design from above
  2. Export at these exact sizes:
    • iOS: 1024×1024px (PNG, no transparency)
    • Android: 512×512px (PNG with transparency OK)
  3. Use online tools to generate all required sizes:
    • makeappicon.com - Free icon generator
    • appicon.co - Generates for all platforms
    • iconkit.app - macOS app for icon sets
  4. Test icons at small sizes (20×20) for clarity
  5. Ensure good contrast against both light and dark backgrounds
