StackMap

Supporting Special Needs Families, One Routine at a Time

Our Story

StackMap was born from a simple belief: every family navigating special needs deserves access to quality tools that make daily life easier. As developers and parents, we understand the unique challenges that special needs families face every day.

Our mission is simple: Keep StackMap free and accessible for every family who needs it. We believe that financial constraints should never prevent a family from accessing tools that can help their children thrive.

We've designed StackMap specifically for special needs and autistic children, with features like:

  • Visual routine cards that provide structure and predictability
  • Large, touch-friendly interfaces for children with motor challenges
  • Celebration animations that encourage and motivate
  • Offline functionality for consistent access to routines
  • Simple, parent-friendly controls

Supporting StackMap

We want to be absolutely clear: contributions are never expected or required. StackMap will always remain free for families who need it. Your use of the app and feedback are contributions enough.

However, if StackMap has been helpful to your family and you find yourself in a position where you'd like to contribute financially, your support helps us:

  • Keep the servers running and the app available 24/7
  • Continue developing new features requested by families
  • Maintain the highest security and privacy standards
  • Keep StackMap free for families who cannot afford to contribute
