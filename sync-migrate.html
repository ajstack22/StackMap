Important Security Update

Your sync data is safe! We've improved how sync links work for better security.

The old sync URL format is no longer supported to protect your privacy.

Your Recovery Phrase

Save this phrase securely. You'll need it to re-enable sync in the app.

How to Re-enable Sync:

  1. Open StackMap on your device
  2. Tap "Grown-ups click here" to enter edit mode
  3. Tap the plus (+) button
  4. Select "Data & Sync"
  5. Choose "Enable Sync"
  6. Select "Join Existing Sync"
  7. Enter your recovery phrase from above
Why this change? The new system ensures your recovery phrase is never sent to our servers, providing true end-to-end encryption for your family's data.
